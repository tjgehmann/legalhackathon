## Pick Your Challenge

Below are **17 challenges** inspired by real legal headaches. Pick one that speaks to you. Work solo or grab a team. There's no wrong answer — just jump in.

---

### 1. Contract Review Assistant
**For:** Legal teams | **Vibe:** "Stop reading the same clause for the 47th time"

Build an AI that reviews vendor contracts, flags risks by severity, and suggests better language. Because lawyers shouldn't spend 90 minutes doing what a machine can do in 90 seconds.

---

### 2. Self-Service NDA Generator
**For:** Sales & Partnerships | **Vibe:** "Just give me the NDA already"

Sales reps wait 5 days for an NDA. Five. Days. Build a tool that asks a few questions and spits out a ready-to-send NDA in under 5 minutes. Legal only gets involved when things get weird.

---

### 3. Legal FAQ Bot
**For:** Everyone | **Vibe:** "Can I use this stock photo or not??"

Every department has legal questions. Most of them have the same answer. Build a bot that answers common questions using actual company policies — in plain language, not legalese.

---

### 4. Clause Library & Comparison Tool
**For:** Legal teams | **Vibe:** "I know we negotiated this before... somewhere"

All that hard-won negotiation knowledge is stuck in people's heads and buried in old files. Build a searchable clause library that compares any clause against your best positions and suggests alternatives.

---

### 5. Compliance Checklist Generator
**For:** Product, Marketing, HR | **Vibe:** "Wait, do we need GDPR consent for this?"

Teams never know which compliance boxes to tick. Build a tool that asks about the project and generates a tailored checklist — not a 50-page memo, but actual steps with priorities.

---

### 6. Legal Document Summarizer & Risk Flagger
**For:** Legal & Management | **Vibe:** "TL;DR this regulation for me"

Turn 30-page legal documents into 1-page summaries with risk ratings and action items. Bonus: make it smart enough to tailor the summary for different audiences (CEO vs. compliance team).

---

### 7. German Court Case Checker
**For:** Legal teams | **Vibe:** "Did the BGH just ruin our business model?"

Track relevant German court decisions automatically. Paste a ruling, get a plain-language breakdown of what it means for your company and whether you need to do something about it.

---

### 8. Vendor Due Diligence Screener
**For:** Compliance & Procurement | **Vibe:** "Are we about to do business with a sanctioned entity?"

Screen vendors against EU, OFAC, UN, and German sanctions lists. Smart name matching, clear risk classifications, and audit-ready reports. Turn 60 minutes of manual checking into seconds.

---

### 9. AGB & Einkaufsbedingungen Review Tool
**For:** Legal & Procurement | **Vibe:** "Is this clause even legal under BGB?"

German AGB law is strict — clauses that look fine can be void under BGB ss 305-310. Build a tool that reviews terms clause-by-clause, maps them to the law, and flags what's risky or outright invalid.

---

### 10. Regulatory Horizon Map
**For:** Executives & Legal | **Vibe:** "Can we launch in June or not?"

Executives don't want 50-page regulatory memos. They want: Go or No-Go. Build a visual decision tool that turns regulation soup into a clear heat map with concrete action items.

---

### 11. Contract Bottleneck & Deal Velocity Dashboard
**For:** Sales, Legal, CFO | **Vibe:** "Why does every deal take 23 days to close?"

The same 5 negotiation points cause 80% of contract delays. Build a dashboard that shows what's slowing deals down, how much money it's costing, and what to pre-approve to speed things up.

---

### 12. Sales Contract Review Playbook
**For:** Sales & Legal | **Vibe:** "Can I accept this redline or do I need to call legal... again?"

Sales reps don't know contract law (and shouldn't have to). Build a playbook that classifies customer redlines as Accept / Negotiate / Escalate, explains the risk, and gives copy-paste counter-proposals.

---

### 13. Customer Complaint Resolution Assistant
**For:** Customer Care & Legal | **Vibe:** "Is this an angry email or a GDPR request?"

200+ complaints a month, handled inconsistently. Build an assistant that classifies complaints, suggests the right response, drafts replies, and catches the legally sensitive ones before they slip through.

---

### 14. Employment Law Self-Service Assistant for HR
**For:** HR & Legal | **Vibe:** "Can we fire this person?" "It depends." "ON WHAT?!"

HR needs to make legally sound decisions fast — dismissals, warnings, fixed-term contracts, Works Council consultations. Build a tool that walks them through the right steps and flags protected employees before it's too late.

---

### 15. Law Enforcement Request Compliance Assistant
**For:** Legal, Compliance, IT | **Vibe:** "The police emailed us. Now what?"

Law enforcement requests come by fax, post, email, and phone. Different rules for each type. Get it wrong and you either violate DSGVO or obstruct justice. Build a tool that classifies, validates, and drafts the right response — every time.

---

### 16. CC Authority Matrix & Resolution Engine
**For:** Customer Care & Legal | **Vibe:** "Do I really need legal sign-off for a EUR 50 refund?"

75% of CC-to-Legal escalations get the same pre-approved answer. Build a system that gives agents "Pre-Approved Legal Lanes" — Green/Yellow/Red classification, instant micro-waivers, and a feedback dashboard that spots systemic issues.

---

### 17. Negotiation Preparation Tool — ZOPA, BATNA & Counterparty Interests
**For:** Legal, Deal Teams, Procurement | **Vibe:** "We're going into this negotiation blind. Again."

Most negotiations are won or lost before the first meeting — yet lawyers walk in without a clear BATNA, a realistic ZOPA, or a structured read of what the other side actually needs. Build an AI-powered prep tool that maps your walk-away points, estimates the counterparty's real interests, surfaces value trades, and generates a principled negotiation strategy (Fisher & Ury style). Win more deals. Walk away from the right ones.

## What Can You Build?

Anything. Seriously. Here are some formats people have used:

- **Slides** — walk us through your concept
- **A working demo** — built with AI tools right here today
- **A process description** — how would this work in real life?
- **A prompt prototype** — the right prompt is half the solution
- **A working paper** — deep analysis with recommendations
- **A hybrid** — mix and match however you want
