# TechForward Solutions GmbH — Standard Contractual Positions

This document outlines TechForward's preferred positions on key contract terms. Use this as the benchmark when reviewing vendor and service provider contracts.

---

## 1. Liability Cap

**Our Standard Position:**
- Liability cap should be tied to **12 months** of fees paid or payable under the agreement
- Mutual liability cap (applies equally to both parties)
- Carve-outs from the cap for: (a) breaches of confidentiality, (b) data protection breaches, (c) IP infringement indemnification, (d) gross negligence or willful misconduct

**Acceptable Range:**
- Minimum: 6 months of fees
- We will push back on anything less than 6 months or that only limits the vendor's liability

**Red Flags:**
- Liability cap tied to fees in the 3 months preceding the event (too low)
- Liability cap that only applies to one party
- No carve-outs for data breaches or confidentiality breaches

---

## 2. Indemnification

**Our Standard Position:**
- Mutual indemnification obligations
- Vendor must indemnify us for IP infringement claims related to the service/deliverables
- Vendor must indemnify us for data breaches caused by vendor's negligence
- We indemnify vendor for claims arising from our materials/data provided to vendor

**Acceptable Range:**
- At minimum, vendor must provide IP infringement indemnification
- We may accept limited indemnification scope if other protections are strong

**Red Flags:**
- One-way indemnification where only the customer indemnifies the vendor
- No IP infringement indemnification from the vendor
- Vendor indemnification capped at an unreasonably low amount

---

## 3. Intellectual Property & Work Product

**Our Standard Position:**
- For custom work/deliverables: **Customer owns all work product** created specifically for us
- For SaaS/licensed products: We acknowledge vendor's ownership but require broad usage rights
- Vendor may retain rights to pre-existing IP and generic tools/methodologies
- Feedback should not automatically transfer to vendor — at most, a non-exclusive license

**Acceptable Range:**
- Joint ownership with unrestricted license to us
- Vendor ownership with perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free license to us

**Red Flags:**
- Vendor owns all work product with only a limited license to us
- We lose rights to work product if agreement terminates
- Automatic assignment of all feedback/suggestions to vendor

---

## 4. Data Protection & Security

**Our Standard Position:**
- Vendor processes data only on our instructions (standard controller-processor relationship)
- Subprocessor changes require prior written notice and right to object
- Data must be processed within EU/EEA or in countries with adequate data protection, with appropriate transfer mechanisms (SCCs) for any transfers outside
- Breach notification within **48 hours** (maximum 72 hours)
- Vendor liable for data breaches without artificial caps below the general liability cap
- Data deletion/return within 30 days of termination

**Acceptable Range:**
- 72-hour breach notification is acceptable
- Subprocessor list publication (rather than individual notification) may be acceptable if right to object is preserved

**Red Flags:**
- Blanket consent to international data transfers without safeguards
- No subprocessor notification or approval rights
- Breach notification liability exclusions
- Data retention exceeding 90 days post-termination

---

## 5. Term, Renewal & Termination

**Our Standard Position:**
- Initial term: 12–24 months (avoid terms longer than 24 months for new vendors)
- Auto-renewal for successive **12-month** periods
- Non-renewal notice: **60 days** before end of term
- Termination for cause: 30-day cure period for both parties
- Termination for convenience: 90-day notice, no penalty (or reasonable wind-down fee)
- Data export period of at least 60 days post-termination

**Acceptable Range:**
- Initial terms up to 36 months if pricing justifies it
- Non-renewal notice up to 90 days is acceptable
- Reasonable early termination fees for convenience termination

**Red Flags:**
- Auto-renewal periods longer than 12 months
- Non-renewal notice requirements exceeding 90 days
- No termination-for-convenience right
- Early termination fees exceeding remaining contract value
- Data export window less than 30 days

---

## 6. Fee Adjustments & Payment Terms

**Our Standard Position:**
- Fee increases capped at **5% per year** or CPI, whichever is lower
- Payment terms: **30 days** from receipt of correct invoice
- Invoices must be detailed and itemized
- Right to dispute invoices within 30 days without late payment penalties on disputed amounts
- No non-refundable / non-cancellable fee commitments (or limited to first year)

**Acceptable Range:**
- Fee increases up to 8% per year
- Payment terms of 15–30 days
- Invoice dispute window of at least 15 days

**Red Flags:**
- Fee increases above 10% per year
- Payment terms shorter than 15 days
- Late payment interest exceeding 1% per month
- All fees non-refundable and non-cancellable
- Very short invoice dispute windows (less than 10 days)

---

## 7. Confidentiality

**Our Standard Position:**
- Mutual confidentiality obligations
- Confidentiality period: 3–5 years post-termination (or indefinite for trade secrets)
- Disclosure to third parties only with prior written consent or under NDA
- No use of our name/logo in marketing without prior written consent

**Acceptable Range:**
- Confidentiality period of at least 2 years post-termination
- Disclosure to affiliates under similar confidentiality obligations

**Red Flags:**
- Confidentiality period of 1 year or less
- Broad right to share confidential information with subcontractors without notice
- Right to use our name/logo without consent

---

## 8. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

**Our Standard Position:**
- Governing law: **German law** (as we are a German entity)
- Dispute resolution: Courts of Berlin, Germany, or ICC arbitration with seat in Berlin
- Language: English

**Acceptable Range:**
- For US vendors: We may accept New York or Delaware law if the contract has strong substantive protections
- Arbitration in a neutral venue is acceptable
- LCIA or ICC arbitration rules preferred

**Red Flags:**
- Exclusive jurisdiction in vendor's home state with unfavorable consumer/business laws
- Mandatory arbitration with no option for injunctive relief in courts
- Waiver of jury trial (less relevant for German entity, but notable)

---

## 9. Force Majeure

**Our Standard Position:**
- Mutual force majeure clause
- Applies to both parties' performance obligations (including payment, if extended)
- Right to terminate if force majeure continues beyond 90 days
- Vendor must have business continuity plans

**Red Flags:**
- Force majeure only benefits the vendor
- Customer payment obligations explicitly excluded from force majeure
- No right to terminate during extended force majeure

---

## 10. Assignment & Change of Control

**Our Standard Position:**
- Neither party may assign without consent
- Exception: assignment to affiliates with prior notice
- Right to terminate if vendor is acquired by a competitor

**Red Flags:**
- Vendor can freely assign without consent
- No notification requirement for change of control
- No termination right upon vendor's change of control
