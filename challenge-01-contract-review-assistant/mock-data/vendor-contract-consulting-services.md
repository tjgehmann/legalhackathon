## 1. SCOPE OF SERVICES

1.1 Consultant shall provide the consulting services described in each Statement of Work ("SOW") executed by the parties. Each SOW shall reference this Agreement and shall be subject to its terms.

1.2 Consultant shall perform the Services using personnel with appropriate qualifications and experience. However, Consultant reserves the right to substitute personnel at any time without Client's approval.

1.3 Consultant shall perform the Services at such locations as Consultant deems appropriate, which may include Consultant's offices, Client's premises, or offshore delivery centers.

1.4 Client acknowledges that time estimates provided in any SOW are good-faith estimates only and are not binding commitments. Consultant shall not be liable for any delays in delivery.

## 2. FEES AND EXPENSES

2.1 Client shall pay Consultant the fees set forth in each SOW. Unless otherwise specified, Consultant's fees are based on time and materials at the rates set forth in Exhibit A.

2.2 Fees do not include travel, lodging, meals, or other out-of-pocket expenses, which shall be reimbursed by Client at cost plus a 15% administrative markup.

2.3 Consultant shall invoice Client monthly. All invoices are due and payable within fifteen (15) days of receipt.

2.4 If Client fails to pay any undisputed amount when due, Consultant may (a) suspend performance of the Services immediately, and (b) charge interest at 2% per month on all overdue amounts.

2.5 In the event of early termination by Client for any reason, Client shall pay for all Services performed through the date of termination plus a termination fee equal to 50% of the remaining fees under all active SOWs.

## 3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

3.1 All work product, deliverables, inventions, and materials created by Consultant in the performance of the Services ("Work Product") shall be the sole and exclusive property of Consultant.

3.2 Upon full payment of all Fees, Consultant grants Client a non-exclusive, non-transferable, limited license to use the Work Product solely for Client's internal business purposes.

3.3 Consultant retains the right to reuse any methodologies, frameworks, tools, know-how, techniques, and generic components developed during the engagement for other clients and purposes.

3.4 Consultant may include a description of the Services performed for Client in Consultant's marketing materials, proposals, and case studies, provided that Confidential Information is not disclosed.

## 4. CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 Each party agrees not to disclose the other party's Confidential Information to any third party without the disclosing party's prior written consent.

4.2 The obligation of confidentiality shall survive for a period of one (1) year following termination of this Agreement.

4.3 Notwithstanding the foregoing, Consultant may share Client's Confidential Information with its subcontractors and offshore team members as necessary to perform the Services.

## 8. TERM AND TERMINATION

8.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of twenty-four (24) months.

8.2 This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

8.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement for cause upon thirty (30) days' written notice of a material breach if the breach remains uncured during such notice period.

8.4 Client may terminate this Agreement or any SOW for convenience upon forty-five (45) days' written notice, subject to the early termination fee set forth in Section 2.5.

## 9. NON-SOLICITATION

9.1 During the term of this Agreement and for twenty-four (24) months following termination, Client shall not directly or indirectly solicit, recruit, or hire any employee or contractor of Consultant who participated in the performance of Services under this Agreement.

9.2 In the event of a breach of Section 9.1, Client shall pay Consultant liquidated damages equal to 100% of the hired individual's first-year annual compensation.

## 10. DATA PROTECTION

10.1 To the extent Consultant processes personal data in connection with the Services, Client shall be responsible for ensuring that all necessary consents and legal bases for processing have been obtained.

10.2 Consultant shall process personal data in accordance with Client's reasonable instructions. Consultant may use subprocessors located in any jurisdiction, including countries that may not provide an adequate level of data protection under applicable law.

10.3 Consultant shall implement industry-standard security measures to protect personal data. The specific security measures shall be at Consultant's sole discretion.

## 11. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

11.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York.

11.2 Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be submitted to binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association in New York City. The arbitration shall be conducted by a single arbitrator selected by the AAA. The arbitrator's decision shall be final and binding.

11.3 The arbitrator shall not have authority to award punitive damages or to make any award that is inconsistent with the limitations set forth in this Agreement.

## 12. GENERAL PROVISIONS

12.1 **Independent Contractor.** Consultant is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, or agency relationship.

12.2 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's written consent, except that Consultant may assign this Agreement to an affiliate or successor entity without Client's consent.

12.3 **Subcontracting.** Consultant may subcontract any portion of the Services without Client's prior approval.

12.4 **Insurance.** Consultant shall maintain professional liability insurance with coverage of at least $1,000,000 per claim and $2,000,000 in the aggregate during the term and for twelve (12) months following termination.

12.5 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement and all SOWs constitute the entire agreement between the parties.
