## 1. Services

**1.1 Scope of Services.** Service Provider agrees to perform the services described in Schedule A attached to this Agreement ("Services") in accordance with the terms and conditions set out herein.

**1.2 Change Orders.** Any changes to the scope, timeline, or fees for the Services must be agreed in writing by both Parties prior to implementation ("Change Order"). A Change Order becomes part of this Agreement upon signature by authorized representatives of both Parties.

**1.3 Standard of Care.** Service Provider shall perform the Services with the degree of skill, care, and diligence reasonably expected of a qualified professional experienced in the relevant field.

**1.4 Personnel.** Service Provider shall assign suitably qualified personnel to perform the Services. [OPTIONAL: Key personnel are identified in Schedule A. Service Provider shall not replace key personnel without TechForward's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.]

**1.5 Subcontracting.** Service Provider shall not subcontract any part of the Services without TechForward's prior written consent.

---

## 2. Fees and Payment

**2.1 Fees.** TechForward shall pay Service Provider the fees set forth in Schedule A in consideration for the satisfactory performance of the Services.

**2.2 Invoicing.** Service Provider shall issue invoices in accordance with the schedule set out in Schedule A. All invoices must reference this Agreement and include sufficient detail to identify the Services performed.

**2.3 Payment Terms.** TechForward shall pay undisputed invoices within **[30 / 45 / 60] calendar days** of receipt of a valid invoice.

**2.4 Expenses.** Out-of-pocket expenses incurred by Service Provider are reimbursable only if: (a) pre-approved in writing by TechForward; and (b) supported by receipts. Approved expenses will be reimbursed at cost.

**2.5 Disputed Invoices.** TechForward shall notify Service Provider in writing within **15 business days** of receiving an invoice if it disputes any amount, specifying the nature of the dispute. The Parties shall cooperate in good faith to resolve disputes promptly. TechForward shall pay any undisputed portion by the due date.

**2.6 Taxes.** Each Party is responsible for its own income taxes. Fees are exclusive of VAT (Umsatzsteuer), which shall be added where applicable and paid by TechForward against a proper VAT invoice.

---

## 3. Term and Termination

**3.1 Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until **[SPECIFIC END DATE / completion of all Services described in Schedule A]**, unless earlier terminated in accordance with this section.

**3.2 Termination for Convenience.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon **[30 / 60 / 90] calendar days'** prior written notice to the other Party.

**3.3 Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party:
(a) materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within **30 calendar days** of receiving written notice specifying the breach in reasonable detail; or
(b) becomes insolvent, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, or is subject to insolvency proceedings that are not dismissed within 60 days.

**3.4 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination or expiry:
(a) TechForward shall pay for all Services satisfactorily performed and expenses approved up to the effective date of termination;
(b) Service Provider shall promptly deliver to TechForward all Work Product, deliverables, and materials in progress;
(c) each Party shall promptly return or securely destroy the other Party's Confidential Information and certify such return or destruction in writing;
(d) Sections 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10 survive termination or expiry.

## 4. Intellectual Property

**4.1 Work Product Ownership.** All work product, deliverables, reports, inventions, developments, and materials created or developed by Service Provider specifically for TechForward under this Agreement ("Work Product") shall be the sole and exclusive property of TechForward from the moment of creation. Service Provider hereby irrevocably assigns to TechForward all rights, title, and interest (including all intellectual property rights) in and to the Work Product.

**4.2 Moral Rights.** To the extent permitted by applicable law, Service Provider waives any moral rights in the Work Product in favor of TechForward and its successors.

**4.3 Background IP.** Each Party retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property and tools ("Background IP"). Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of either Party's Background IP.

**4.4 License to Use Background IP.** Service Provider grants TechForward a non-exclusive, royalty-free, perpetual license to use Service Provider's Background IP to the extent it is incorporated in or necessary for TechForward to use the Work Product.

**4.5 License for Performance.** TechForward grants Service Provider a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable license to access and use TechForward's materials, systems, and data solely to the extent necessary to perform the Services during the term of this Agreement.

## 5. Confidentiality

**5.1 Confidential Information.** "Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other (the "Receiving Party") in connection with this Agreement, whether disclosed orally, in writing, or in any other form, that is designated as confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information and circumstances of disclosure.

**5.2 Obligations.** The Receiving Party shall: (a) hold the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) not disclose it to any third party without the Disclosing Party's prior written consent; (c) use it solely for the purpose of performing its obligations or exercising its rights under this Agreement; and (d) protect it using at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information, and not less than reasonable care.

**5.3 Permitted Disclosures.** The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to its employees, contractors, and advisors who: (a) have a need to know it for the purposes of this Agreement; and (b) are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this Agreement.

**5.4 Exceptions.** Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes publicly known without breach of this Agreement; (b) was already known to the Receiving Party at the time of disclosure; (c) is received from a third party without restriction; (d) is independently developed without use of Confidential Information; or (e) must be disclosed by applicable law, regulation, or court order — in which case the Receiving Party shall provide prompt prior written notice to the Disclosing Party where legally permitted.

**5.5 Duration.** Confidentiality obligations under this section survive termination or expiry of this Agreement for **[2 / 3] years**.

## 6. Data Protection

**6.1 Compliance.** Each Party shall comply with all applicable data protection and privacy laws, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and the German Federal Data Protection Act (BDSG) where applicable.

**6.2 Data Processing Agreement.** If Service Provider processes personal data on behalf of TechForward in the course of performing the Services, the Parties shall execute a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) in accordance with Article 28 GDPR prior to any such processing. The DPA forms an integral part of this Agreement.

**6.3 Security.** Service Provider shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data and TechForward's Confidential Information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

## 7. Representations and Warranties

**7.1 Mutual.** Each Party represents and warrants that: (a) it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement; (b) this Agreement constitutes a valid and binding obligation; and (c) entering into and performing this Agreement does not violate any other agreement, obligation, or applicable law.

**7.2 Service Provider.** Service Provider additionally represents and warrants that: (a) the Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner, consistent with industry standards; (b) the Work Product will not infringe, misappropriate, or violate any third-party intellectual property rights; (c) Service Provider has the right to assign the Work Product to TechForward as provided in Section 4; and (d) Service Provider will comply with all applicable laws and regulations in performing the Services.

---

## 8. Limitation of Liability

**8.1 Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, neither Party shall be liable to the other for indirect, incidental, special, exemplary, punitive, or consequential damages — including loss of profits, loss of revenue, loss of data, or business interruption — even if advised of the possibility of such damages.

**8.2 Aggregate Cap.** Each Party's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed **EUR [CAP AMOUNT — default: total fees paid or payable in the 12 months preceding the event giving rise to the claim]**.

**8.3 Exceptions.** The limitations in Sections 8.1 and 8.2 shall not apply to liability arising from: (a) death or personal injury caused by a Party's negligence; (b) fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation; (c) breach of Section 5 (Confidentiality); (d) infringement of intellectual property rights; (e) a Party's gross negligence or willful misconduct; or (f) any liability that cannot be excluded or limited under applicable law.

---

## 9. Indemnification

**9.1 By Service Provider.** Service Provider shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless TechForward and its affiliates, officers, directors, and employees from and against any third-party claims, damages, losses, and expenses (including reasonable legal fees) arising from: (a) Service Provider's negligence or willful misconduct in performing the Services; (b) infringement of third-party intellectual property rights by the Work Product; or (c) Service Provider's material breach of its representations, warranties, or obligations under this Agreement.

**9.2 By TechForward.** TechForward shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Service Provider from and against any third-party claims arising solely from TechForward's use of the Work Product in a manner that materially deviates from the intended use described in Schedule A.

**9.3 Indemnification Procedure.** The Party seeking indemnification shall: (a) promptly notify the indemnifying Party in writing of the claim; (b) grant the indemnifying Party sole control of the defense and settlement (provided no settlement imposes obligations on the indemnified Party without its consent); and (c) provide reasonable cooperation and assistance.

## 10. General Provisions

**10.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of **[GOVERNING LAW, default: the Federal Republic of Germany]**, excluding its conflict of law rules.

**10.2 Dispute Resolution.** The Parties shall attempt to resolve any dispute arising from or in connection with this Agreement through good-faith negotiations for a period of **30 calendar days** following written notice from either Party. If the dispute is not resolved within that period, it shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of **[JURISDICTION, default: Munich, Germany]**.

**10.3 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with all Schedules and any Change Orders, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, warranties, and agreements relating to the same subject matter.

**10.4 Amendment.** This Agreement may only be modified by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties. No purchase order, click-through terms, or other standard form issued by either Party shall amend this Agreement.

**10.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

**10.6 Waiver.** Failure by either Party to enforce any provision of this Agreement shall not constitute a waiver of that Party's rights to enforce that provision or any other provision in the future.

**10.7 Notices.** Notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by: (a) email with written confirmation of receipt; (b) courier with tracking; or (c) registered mail with return receipt — to the addresses set forth in Schedule A (Notices) or as updated by written notice.

**10.8 Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. This Agreement does not create any employment, agency, partnership, joint venture, or fiduciary relationship between the Parties.

**10.9 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign or transfer any rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that TechForward may assign this Agreement without consent to an affiliate or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or substantially all of its assets.

**10.10 Force Majeure.** Neither Party shall be in breach or liable for delay or failure to perform obligations to the extent caused by events beyond its reasonable control (including acts of God, war, terrorism, pandemic, or government action), provided the affected Party promptly notifies the other and uses reasonable efforts to mitigate the impact.

**10.11 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be signed in counterparts (including electronic signatures), each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together constitute one and the same instrument.
