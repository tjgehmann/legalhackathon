## Section 1: Intellectual Property Policy

### 1.1 Company Ownership of Work Product
All inventions, works of authorship, designs, software, data, and other materials created by employees in the course of their employment are the property of TechForward. This includes work created during working hours, using company resources, or relating to the company's business.

### 1.2 Use of Third-Party Content
- **Stock photos and images:** Employees may use images from TechForward's licensed stock photo accounts (currently Shutterstock and Adobe Stock). Check with the Marketing team for login credentials. Do NOT download images from Google Image Search or other websites without verifying the license.
- **Open-source software:** Open-source software may be used in company products subject to the following:
  - **Permissive licenses** (MIT, BSD, Apache 2.0): Approved for use. Notify the engineering lead.
  - **Copyleft licenses** (GPL, LGPL, AGPL): Require legal approval before use. Submit a request via the Legal Service Desk.
  - **Creative Commons:** CC0 and CC-BY are approved. CC-BY-SA, CC-BY-NC, and CC-BY-ND require legal review.
- **Third-party trademarks:** Do not use another company's logo or trademark without their written permission. Factual references to other companies by name are generally acceptable (e.g., "Compatible with Salesforce").

### 1.3 Customer Logo Usage
- You may use a customer's logo on our website, in presentations, or in marketing materials **only if** there is a written agreement permitting such use (e.g., a logo usage clause in the customer contract or a separate consent).
- Check the CRM for logo usage permissions before using any customer's brand assets.
- When in doubt, ask the customer directly. Marketing maintains a list of approved customer logos.

### 1.4 Inventions and Patents
- If you invent something that may be patentable, report it to the Legal team using the Invention Disclosure Form.
- Do not file patent applications or discuss potential patents externally without legal approval.

## Section 2: Data Privacy & Protection Policy

### 2.1 General Principles
TechForward is committed to protecting the personal data of employees, customers, and partners in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable data protection laws.

### 2.2 Lawful Basis for Processing
Before collecting or processing personal data, ensure you have a valid lawful basis:
- **Consent:** The individual has given clear consent (must be freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous)
- **Contract:** Processing is necessary to fulfill a contract with the individual
- **Legal obligation:** Processing is required by law
- **Legitimate interest:** Processing is necessary for legitimate business purposes, provided it does not override the individual's rights

### 2.3 Sharing Personal Data
- **Within TechForward:** Personal data may be shared between departments on a need-to-know basis for legitimate business purposes.
- **With vendors/partners:** Personal data may only be shared with external parties if:
  (a) A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is in place, AND
  (b) The transfer complies with GDPR cross-border transfer requirements (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers outside the EU/EEA)
- **With customers' partners:** Do NOT share customer data with the customer's partners or affiliates unless explicitly authorized by the customer in writing.

### 2.4 Data Subject Rights
Individuals have the right to access, correct, delete, and port their personal data. If you receive a data subject request, forward it immediately to the Data Protection Officer (DPO) at dpo@techforward.example.com. Do not attempt to respond directly.

### 2.5 Data Breach Procedures
If you suspect a data breach (unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure of personal data):
1. Report it to the DPO immediately (within 1 hour of discovery)
2. Do not attempt to investigate or contain the breach yourself
3. Preserve all evidence
4. Do not communicate about the breach externally

### 2.6 Data Retention
- Customer data: Retained for the duration of the customer contract plus 12 months, then deleted
- Employee data: Retained for the duration of employment plus the legally required retention period (varies by data type and jurisdiction)
- Marketing data: Consent-based data deleted within 30 days of consent withdrawal

## Section 3: Contract Policy

### 3.1 Who Can Sign Contracts
- Contracts with a total value up to **EUR 50,000**: Department heads may sign
- Contracts between **EUR 50,001 and EUR 500,000**: VP-level approval and Legal review required
- Contracts above **EUR 500,000**: CFO or CEO signature required, plus Legal review
- **NDAs:** May be signed by department heads regardless of value, provided TechForward's standard template is used without modification

### 3.2 Legal Review Requirements
The following contracts **always** require legal review, regardless of value:
- Contracts involving personal data processing (vendor DPAs)
- Contracts with non-standard liability or indemnification terms
- Contracts with government entities
- Contracts involving IP licensing or assignment
- Employment contracts and contractor agreements
- Lease and real estate agreements

### 3.3 Standard Templates
TechForward maintains standard templates for common agreements. Use the standard template whenever possible:
- Mutual NDA
- One-way NDA
- Vendor/supplier agreement
- Customer subscription agreement
- Consulting services agreement
- Data Processing Agreement

Templates are available on the Legal SharePoint site. If a counterparty insists on using their own paper, the contract must be reviewed by Legal.

### 3.4 Contract Storage
All executed contracts must be uploaded to the Contract Management System (Juro) within 5 business days of execution. Include:
- Executed PDF
- Key metadata (parties, value, start/end dates, renewal terms)
- Assigned contract owner

## Section 4: Employment Law Basics

### 4.1 Hiring and Offers
- All job offers must be reviewed by HR before being extended
- Offer letters must use the HR-approved template
- Do not make verbal promises about compensation, equity, or role scope that are not reflected in the written offer
- Background checks are conducted for all new hires. Do not allow new employees to start before the background check is complete.

### 4.2 Employee Referral Program
- Employees may refer candidates for open positions through the referral portal
- Referral bonuses are: EUR 3,000 for individual contributor roles, EUR 5,000 for management roles, EUR 7,500 for director-level and above
- Bonuses are paid 50% upon the new hire's start date and 50% after 6 months of employment
- HR, Legal, and Finance team members are not eligible for referral bonuses for positions in their own department

### 4.3 Termination of Employment
- All termination decisions must be reviewed by HR and Legal before communicating to the employee
- Follow the documented performance improvement process before any performance-based termination
- German labor law provides strong employee protections — terminations require valid legal grounds and proper notice periods
- Never discuss termination reasons with other employees

### 4.4 Contractor vs. Employee
- Engaging independent contractors requires legal review to ensure proper classification
- Key factors: control over how work is done, exclusivity, integration into the organization, use of own tools
- Misclassification can result in significant penalties — when in doubt, consult Legal

## Section 5: Compliance & Ethics

### 5.1 Anti-Bribery and Corruption
- TechForward has zero tolerance for bribery and corruption
- Do not offer or accept gifts, hospitality, or entertainment worth more than **EUR 100** per instance without management approval
- Government officials: Even nominal gifts may be prohibited — always consult Legal before offering anything of value
- All gifts given or received above EUR 50 must be logged in the Gift Register

### 5.2 Competition / Antitrust Law
- Do not discuss pricing, market allocation, customer allocation, or bidding strategies with competitors
- Do not enter into agreements with competitors that restrict competition
- If a competitor contacts you about any of these topics, end the conversation immediately and report it to Legal
- Trade association meetings: Follow the guidelines in the Competition Law Handbook before attending

### 5.3 Sanctions and Export Controls
- Do not engage in business with individuals, entities, or countries subject to sanctions (EU, US, UK, or UN sanctions)
- All new customers and vendors are screened against sanctions lists during onboarding
- If you become aware that a business partner may be subject to sanctions, report it to Legal immediately
- Export of technology: Certain software and technical data may be subject to export controls. Consult Legal before sharing technology with parties outside the EU/US.

### 5.4 Whistleblower Protection
- TechForward provides a confidential reporting channel for compliance concerns
- Reports can be made anonymously through the Ethics Hotline
- Retaliation against whistleblowers is strictly prohibited and is itself a terminable offense

## Section 6: Marketing & Communications

### 6.1 Email Marketing
- All marketing emails must include an unsubscribe mechanism
- Only send marketing emails to individuals who have given explicit consent (opt-in)
- Double opt-in is required for EU contacts
- Maintain suppression lists and honor unsubscribe requests within 5 business days

### 6.2 Social Media
- Only authorized communications team members may post on official TechForward social media accounts
- Personal social media: Employees may mention their employment at TechForward but must not share confidential information, speak on behalf of the company, or disparage competitors

### 6.3 Press and Analyst Communications
- All press inquiries must be routed to the Communications team
- Do not speak to journalists or industry analysts about TechForward's business without prior authorization
- Press releases require Legal review before publication

### 6.4 Use of Customer Testimonials
- Customer testimonials and case studies require the customer's written approval before publication
- The marketing team must obtain and document this approval
- Testimonials must be factually accurate and not misleading
