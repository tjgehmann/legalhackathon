# Topics That Should Always Be Escalated to Human Lawyers

The Legal FAQ Bot should **never** attempt to provide definitive answers on the following topics. Instead, it should clearly state that the question requires human legal review and direct the user to the appropriate contact.

---

## 1. Employment Termination & Disciplinary Actions
- Terminating an employee
- Placing an employee on a performance improvement plan
- Disciplinary proceedings
- Workplace harassment or discrimination complaints
- Redundancy / layoff decisions

**Why:** Employment decisions carry significant legal risk, especially under German labor law. Individual circumstances matter enormously, and bad advice can lead to costly litigation.

**Escalate to:** HR Department and Legal Team jointly

---

## 2. Litigation & Disputes
- Any threatened or actual lawsuit
- Demand letters or cease-and-desist notices
- Regulatory investigations or inquiries
- Customer or partner disputes that may escalate to legal action
- Debt collection issues

**Why:** Litigation matters require immediate, privileged legal advice. Responses can create or waive legal rights.

**Escalate to:** Legal Team (General Counsel)

---

## 3. Regulatory Investigations
- Government or regulatory authority inquiries
- Dawn raids or inspections
- Requests for information from regulators
- Competition authority investigations

**Why:** Regulatory matters require specialized handling to protect the company's rights and manage privilege.

**Escalate to:** Legal Team (General Counsel) — immediately

---

## 4. Criminal or Fraud Matters
- Suspected fraud, embezzlement, or theft
- Bribery or corruption allegations
- Suspected money laundering
- Any criminal conduct by or against employees

**Why:** Criminal matters require immediate and careful handling, often involving external counsel and law enforcement.

**Escalate to:** Ethics Hotline (for anonymous reporting) or Legal Team directly

---

## 5. M&A and Corporate Transactions
- Potential acquisitions, mergers, or divestitures
- Investment or funding discussions
- Joint venture proposals
- Corporate restructuring

**Why:** Corporate transactions are complex, high-stakes matters that require specialized legal counsel and often involve confidentiality obligations.

**Escalate to:** General Counsel / CFO

---

## 6. Major Data Breaches
- Confirmed breaches affecting large numbers of individuals
- Breaches involving sensitive personal data categories
- Cross-border breach incidents
- Breaches requiring regulatory notification

**Why:** Data breaches have strict notification timelines (72 hours under GDPR) and require coordinated legal, technical, and communications responses.

**Escalate to:** Data Protection Officer (immediately)

---

## 7. Intellectual Property Disputes
- Claims that TechForward is infringing third-party IP
- Discovery that a third party is infringing TechForward's IP
- Patent trolls or frivolous IP claims
- Trade secret misappropriation

**Why:** IP disputes require strategic legal assessment and may involve litigation.

**Escalate to:** Legal Team (IP Counsel)

---

## 8. Sanctions and Export Control Violations
- Suspected dealings with sanctioned parties
- Potential export control violations
- Questions about specific sanctions designations

**Why:** Sanctions violations carry severe penalties (including criminal liability). Any suspected violation requires immediate legal assessment.

**Escalate to:** Legal Team (Compliance) — immediately

---

## Bot Response Template for Escalation

When the bot identifies a question that falls into one of these categories, it should respond with:

> **I can't provide guidance on this topic** — it requires review by a human lawyer who can assess your specific situation.
>
> **What to do:** Contact [APPROPRIATE TEAM/CONTACT] as soon as possible.
>
> **Why this needs human review:** [BRIEF EXPLANATION]
>
> **Urgency:** [STANDARD / HIGH / IMMEDIATE]
