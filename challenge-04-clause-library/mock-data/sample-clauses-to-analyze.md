# Sample Clauses for Analysis

Use these clauses from counterparty contracts to test your clause comparison tool. Each clause should be analyzed against the clause library to determine its favorability and suggest alternatives.

---

## Clause A — Limitation of Liability (from a SaaS vendor)

> IN NO EVENT SHALL PROVIDER'S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT EXCEED THE AMOUNTS PAID BY CUSTOMER DURING THE THREE (3) MONTH PERIOD PRIOR TO THE DATE ON WHICH THE CLAIM AROSE. IN NO EVENT SHALL PROVIDER BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, SPECIAL, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, EXEMPLARY, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, INCLUDING BUT NOT LIMITED TO DAMAGES FOR LOSS OF PROFITS, GOODWILL, USE, DATA, OR OTHER INTANGIBLE LOSSES, REGARDLESS OF WHETHER PROVIDER HAS BEEN ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.

**Context:** This is from a $250,000/year SaaS analytics platform agreement.

---

## Clause B — Indemnification (from a consulting firm)

> Client shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Consultant and its affiliates, officers, directors, employees, agents, and successors from and against any and all third-party claims, damages, losses, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or in connection with: (a) Client's use of the Deliverables; (b) Client's products and services; (c) any materials provided by Client to Consultant; or (d) Client's breach of this Agreement. Consultant's sole indemnification obligation shall be limited to claims that Deliverables directly infringe a registered US patent or copyright, provided that Consultant's aggregate indemnification liability shall not exceed the fees paid under the applicable Statement of Work.

**Context:** This is from a consulting services agreement with an estimated $400,000 value.

---

## Clause C — Data Protection (from a US-based HR platform)

> Customer acknowledges and agrees that Provider may process Customer Data, including personal data of Customer's employees, in the United States and such other countries as Provider deems necessary for the provision of the Service. Provider maintains SOC 2 Type II certification and employs industry-standard security measures. Provider shall notify Customer of any security incident that Provider determines, in its sole discretion, requires notification under applicable law. Customer is solely responsible for obtaining all necessary consents from data subjects for the processing of personal data through the Service.

**Context:** This is from an HR management platform used by TechForward's 200 EU-based employees.

---

## Clause D — Termination (from an infrastructure vendor)

> This Agreement has an initial term of thirty-six (36) months. It shall automatically renew for successive twenty-four (24) month periods unless Customer provides written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred twenty (120) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term. Provider may terminate this Agreement immediately if Customer fails to pay any amount when due or breaches any other provision of this Agreement. Customer may terminate only for Provider's material breach that remains uncured for ninety (90) days after written notice. Upon termination for any reason, all fees for the remainder of the then-current term shall become immediately due and payable. Customer shall have thirty (30) calendar days to export Customer Data following termination.

**Context:** This is from a cloud infrastructure agreement worth $180,000/year.

## Clause E — Intellectual Property (from a design agency)

> All concepts, designs, artwork, copy, and other creative materials developed by Agency in the performance of the Services (collectively, "Creative Work") shall remain the exclusive property of Agency. Upon payment in full of all fees due under the applicable Statement of Work, Agency grants Client a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use the Creative Work solely for the purposes specified in the Statement of Work. Agency may use the Creative Work, including any final designs, in Agency's portfolio and marketing materials. Client shall not modify the Creative Work without Agency's prior written consent.

**Context:** This is from a branding and design engagement worth $75,000. The Creative Work will include TechForward's new brand identity, website design, and marketing collateral.
