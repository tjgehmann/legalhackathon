# COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

**Lease Reference:** CL-2026-0294
**Date:** February 1, 2026

**Landlord:** Meridian Property Holdings GmbH ("Landlord"), Kurfürstendamm 195, 10707 Berlin, Germany

**Tenant:** TechForward Solutions GmbH ("Tenant"), Friedrichstraße 123, 10117 Berlin, Germany

**Premises:** Floors 4, 5, and 6 of the office building located at Alexanderstraße 7, 10178 Berlin, Germany (the "Premises"), comprising approximately 2,800 square meters of usable office space.

---

## 1. GRANT OF LEASE

1.1 Landlord hereby leases to Tenant, and Tenant hereby leases from Landlord, the Premises for the term and upon the conditions set forth in this Agreement.

1.2 The Premises shall be used exclusively for general office purposes, including software development, business administration, and related activities. Any change in use requires Landlord's prior written consent.

## 2. TERM

2.1 The initial lease term shall be ten (10) years, commencing on July 1, 2026, and expiring on June 30, 2036 (the "Initial Term").

2.2 Tenant shall have the option to renew the Lease for two (2) additional periods of five (5) years each (each a "Renewal Term"), provided that Tenant gives written notice of its intention to renew at least twelve (12) months prior to the expiration of the then-current term.

2.3 If Tenant fails to provide timely notice of renewal or non-renewal, the Lease shall continue on a month-to-month basis at 120% of the then-current Base Rent, terminable by either party upon six (6) months' written notice.

## 3. RENT

3.1 **Base Rent.** Tenant shall pay a monthly Base Rent of EUR 78,400 (equivalent to EUR 28.00 per square meter per month), payable in advance on the first business day of each calendar month.

3.2 **Rent Escalation.** The Base Rent shall increase by 3% per annum on each anniversary of the Commencement Date. In addition, every three (3) years, the Base Rent shall be adjusted to reflect the Consumer Price Index (CPI) for Berlin if the cumulative CPI increase exceeds the cumulative contractual increases.

3.3 **Operating Costs.** In addition to Base Rent, Tenant shall pay its proportionate share of the building's operating costs, estimated at EUR 6.50 per square meter per month. Operating costs include building management, cleaning of common areas, security, insurance, property tax, and maintenance of building systems. Landlord shall provide an annual reconciliation of estimated versus actual operating costs.

3.4 **Utilities.** Tenant shall be responsible for all utility costs for the Premises (electricity, water, heating, internet/telecommunications), payable directly to the utility providers or reimbursed to Landlord based on metered usage.

3.5 **Security Deposit.** Tenant shall provide a security deposit equal to six (6) months' Base Rent (EUR 470,400) in the form of a bank guarantee, to be delivered prior to the Commencement Date. The security deposit shall be returned within twelve (12) months following the expiration or termination of the Lease, subject to any deductions for unpaid rent, damages, or restoration costs.

## 4. TENANT IMPROVEMENTS

4.1 Tenant may make non-structural alterations to the Premises at Tenant's expense without Landlord's prior approval, provided that such alterations do not affect the building's structural integrity, mechanical systems, or external appearance.

4.2 Structural alterations, modifications to the building's HVAC, electrical, or plumbing systems, or changes visible from the exterior require Landlord's prior written approval, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

4.3 All improvements and alterations made by Tenant shall become the property of Landlord upon installation. Landlord may require Tenant to remove any or all alterations and restore the Premises to their original condition upon termination of the Lease, at Tenant's expense.

4.4 Tenant shall bear all costs of obtaining permits and approvals for any alterations.

## 5. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

5.1 **Landlord's Obligations.** Landlord shall maintain the structural elements of the building (roof, exterior walls, foundation, and load-bearing structures), common areas, building mechanical systems (elevators, central HVAC, fire safety systems), and the building's external appearance.

5.2 **Tenant's Obligations.** Tenant shall maintain the Premises in good condition and repair, including interior walls, flooring, ceiling tiles, lighting fixtures, interior doors, and any Tenant-installed improvements. Tenant shall be responsible for all repairs necessitated by Tenant's use, negligence, or willful misconduct.

5.3 **Emergency Repairs.** Landlord may enter the Premises without notice to perform emergency repairs to prevent imminent danger to persons or property. For non-emergency repairs, Landlord shall provide at least 48 hours' written notice.

5.4 Tenant shall promptly notify Landlord of any damage to the Premises or building systems that comes to Tenant's attention.

## 6. INSURANCE

6.1 Tenant shall maintain the following insurance coverage throughout the Lease term:
   (a) Commercial general liability insurance with limits of at least EUR 5,000,000 per occurrence
   (b) Property insurance covering Tenant's personal property, fixtures, and improvements
   (c) Business interruption insurance covering at least twelve (12) months of rent
   (d) Workers' compensation insurance as required by law

6.2 Landlord shall be named as an additional insured on Tenant's general liability policy.

6.3 Landlord shall maintain property insurance for the building structure and common areas.

6.4 Each party waives any right of subrogation against the other party to the extent covered by their respective insurance policies.

## 7. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

7.1 Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet any portion of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, delayed, or conditioned.

7.2 Notwithstanding Section 7.1, Tenant may assign this Lease or sublet the Premises to a parent, subsidiary, or affiliate of Tenant, or to a successor entity in connection with a merger, reorganization, or sale of all or substantially all of Tenant's assets, without Landlord's consent, provided that (a) the assignee/subtenant assumes all of Tenant's obligations, and (b) the assignee/subtenant has a net worth at least equal to Tenant's net worth at the time of the transaction.

7.3 In the event of a permitted assignment or subletting, Tenant shall remain primarily liable for all obligations under this Lease unless Landlord expressly releases Tenant in writing.

7.4 If Tenant sublets any portion of the Premises at a rental rate exceeding the per-square-meter rent under this Lease, Landlord shall be entitled to 50% of such excess rental income.

## 8. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

8.1 **Tenant Default.** Each of the following shall constitute an Event of Default:
   (a) Tenant fails to pay rent or any other amount due within fifteen (15) days of the due date;
   (b) Tenant breaches any non-monetary provision and fails to cure within thirty (30) days of written notice (or such longer period as reasonably necessary if the breach cannot be cured within 30 days, provided Tenant diligently pursues the cure);
   (c) Tenant becomes insolvent, files for bankruptcy, or has a receiver appointed for its assets;
   (d) Tenant abandons the Premises.

8.2 **Landlord's Remedies.** Upon an Event of Default, Landlord may, at its option:
   (a) terminate this Lease upon written notice and recover possession of the Premises;
   (b) maintain this Lease in effect and recover rent as it becomes due;
   (c) re-enter the Premises and relet them on Tenant's behalf, applying the proceeds to Tenant's obligations, with Tenant remaining liable for any deficiency;
   (d) pursue any other remedy available at law or in equity.

8.3 **Landlord Default.** If Landlord fails to perform any obligation under this Lease and such failure continues for thirty (30) days after written notice from Tenant (or such longer period as reasonably necessary), Tenant may (a) cure the default and offset the reasonable costs against future rent, or (b) terminate the Lease if the default materially impairs Tenant's use of the Premises.

8.4 **Late Payment.** Late payments shall bear interest at 5% per annum above the European Central Bank base rate, calculated from the due date until payment.

## 9. DAMAGE AND DESTRUCTION

9.1 If the Premises are partially damaged by fire or other casualty but remain substantially usable, Landlord shall repair the damage within a reasonable time. Rent shall be proportionally abated based on the area rendered unusable.

9.2 If the Premises are totally destroyed or damaged to such an extent that they cannot be restored within twelve (12) months, either party may terminate this Lease upon thirty (30) days' written notice.

9.3 Landlord shall not be obligated to repair or replace Tenant's personal property, fixtures, or improvements.

## 10. ENVIRONMENTAL

10.1 Tenant shall not use, generate, store, or dispose of any hazardous materials on the Premises, except for ordinary office supplies in quantities typical for general office use and in compliance with all applicable environmental laws.

10.2 Tenant shall indemnify Landlord from any claims, costs, or liabilities arising from Tenant's introduction of hazardous materials to the Premises.

10.3 Landlord represents that, to the best of its knowledge, the Premises and the building are free from hazardous materials contamination as of the Commencement Date.

## 11. QUIET ENJOYMENT

11.1 Provided Tenant performs its obligations under this Lease, Landlord covenants that Tenant shall have quiet and peaceful enjoyment of the Premises during the Lease term without interference from Landlord or any party claiming through Landlord.

## 12. FORCE MAJEURE

12.1 Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform its obligations (other than monetary obligations) if such failure results from circumstances beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, war, terrorism, pandemic, government orders, natural disaster, fire, flood, or labor disputes.

12.2 If a force majeure event prevents performance for more than one hundred eighty (180) days, either party may terminate this Lease upon thirty (30) days' written notice.

## 13. NOTICES

13.1 All notices under this Lease shall be in writing and delivered by registered mail, internationally recognized courier service, or personal delivery to the addresses set forth in the preamble.

13.2 Notices shall be deemed delivered (a) upon receipt if delivered personally, (b) three (3) business days after mailing by registered mail, or (c) one (1) business day after deposit with an internationally recognized courier service.

## 14. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

14.1 This Lease shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Federal Republic of Germany.

14.2 Any disputes arising under or in connection with this Lease shall be resolved exclusively by the courts of Berlin, Germany.

## 15. SURRENDER

15.1 Upon expiration or termination of this Lease, Tenant shall surrender the Premises in good condition and repair, reasonable wear and tear excepted.

15.2 Tenant shall remove all personal property and, if requested by Landlord, any alterations or improvements, and restore the Premises to their original condition. Items not removed within thirty (30) days of Lease expiration shall be deemed abandoned and may be disposed of by Landlord at Tenant's expense.

## 16. MISCELLANEOUS

16.1 **Entire Agreement.** This Lease constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior negotiations and agreements.

16.2 **Amendments.** This Lease may only be amended by a written instrument signed by both parties.

16.3 **Severability.** If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force.

16.4 **No Waiver.** Failure by either party to enforce any provision shall not constitute a waiver of such provision.

16.5 **Recording.** Neither party shall record this Lease or any memorandum thereof without the other party's consent.
