# Summary Templates for Different Audiences and Document Types

These templates provide structured formats for legal document summaries. The AI tool should adapt the summary format to both the document type and the target audience.

---

## Template 1: Executive Summary (for C-Suite / Board)

**Use for:** Regulations, court decisions, policy changes, major contracts
**Audience:** CEO, CFO, Board members — need the "so what" in 1 page or less

### Structure

```
EXECUTIVE BRIEF
Document: [Document name and reference]
Date: [Date]
Prepared for: [Audience]

BOTTOM LINE
[2-3 sentences: What is this, why does it matter to us, and what do we need to do?]

KEY POINTS
• [Point 1 — plain language, no jargon]
• [Point 2]
• [Point 3]
• [Point 4 — max 5 points]

BUSINESS IMPACT
• Financial: [Cost implications, fines, budget needs]
• Operational: [Process changes, timeline impacts]
• Strategic: [Market position, competitive implications]

RECOMMENDED ACTIONS
1. [Action] — Owner: [Who] — Deadline: [When]
2. [Action] — Owner: [Who] — Deadline: [When]
3. [Action] — Owner: [Who] — Deadline: [When]

RISK IF NO ACTION
[1-2 sentences on consequences of inaction]
```

**Tone:** Direct, business-focused, no legal jargon. Assume the reader has 3 minutes.

## Template 2: Legal Analysis Summary (for Legal Team)

**Use for:** Regulations, court decisions, opposing party contracts
**Audience:** General Counsel, legal team — need detailed legal analysis

### Structure

```
LEGAL ANALYSIS
Document: [Full document title, reference, jurisdiction]
Analyzed by: [Name/AI tool]
Date of analysis: [Date]

1. OVERVIEW
[3-5 sentence summary of the document's purpose and scope]

2. KEY PROVISIONS / HOLDINGS
[For regulations: key requirements organized by article/section]
[For court decisions: key holdings and reasoning]
[For contracts: key terms organized by topic]

   2.1 [Topic]
       - Provision: [What it says]
       - Analysis: [What it means for us]
       - Risk level: [High / Medium / Low]

   2.2 [Topic]
       ...

3. RISK ASSESSMENT
| Risk | Severity | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|----------|------------|--------|------------|
| [Risk 1] | High/Med/Low | High/Med/Low | [Description] | [Steps] |
| [Risk 2] | ... | ... | ... | ... |

4. COMPARISON TO CURRENT POSITION
[How does this differ from our current practices/contracts/understanding?]

5. ACTION ITEMS
| # | Action | Priority | Owner | Deadline | Legal Sign-off |
|---|--------|----------|-------|----------|----------------|
| 1 | [Action] | [P1/P2/P3] | [Who] | [When] | [Yes/No] |

6. OPEN QUESTIONS
[Questions that require further research or discussion]

7. REFERENCES
[Citations to specific articles, clauses, or page numbers]
```

**Tone:** Precise, analytical, cite specific provisions. Assume the reader is a qualified lawyer.

## Template 3: Compliance Impact Brief (for Compliance / Ops Teams)

**Use for:** New regulations, regulatory guidance, policy changes
**Audience:** Compliance officers, operational teams — need to know what to implement

### Structure

```
COMPLIANCE IMPACT BRIEF
Regulation/Document: [Name]
Effective Date: [When requirements take effect]
Applies to: [Which parts of the business]

WHAT'S CHANGING
[3-5 bullet points summarizing the new requirements in plain language]

WHO IS AFFECTED
• [Department/team 1]: [How they're affected]
• [Department/team 2]: [How they're affected]
• [Department/team 3]: [How they're affected]

COMPLIANCE CHECKLIST
□ [Requirement 1] — Deadline: [Date] — Status: [Not started / In progress / Done]
□ [Requirement 2] — Deadline: [Date] — Status: [Not started / In progress / Done]
□ [Requirement 3] — Deadline: [Date] — Status: [Not started / In progress / Done]

IMPLEMENTATION TIMELINE
[Phase 1 — by Date]: [What needs to happen]
[Phase 2 — by Date]: [What needs to happen]
[Phase 3 — by Date]: [What needs to happen]

PENALTIES FOR NON-COMPLIANCE
[What happens if we don't comply — fines, enforcement actions, reputational risk]

RESOURCES NEEDED
• Budget: [Estimated cost]
• People: [FTEs or external resources needed]
• Technology: [System changes or new tools required]

NEXT STEPS
1. [Immediate action]
2. [Short-term action]
3. [Long-term action]
```

**Tone:** Practical, action-oriented, organized by "what to do." Assume the reader needs to build an implementation plan.

## Template 4: Contract Risk Summary (for Business Stakeholders)

**Use for:** Vendor contracts, customer agreements, partnership agreements
**Audience:** Business owners, procurement — need to understand risks before signing

### Structure

```
CONTRACT RISK SUMMARY
Contract: [Name and parties]
Value: [Contract value]
Term: [Duration]
Reviewed by: [Name/AI tool]

OVERALL RISK RATING: [Low / Medium / High / Critical]

DEAL TERMS AT A GLANCE
• Type: [SaaS / Services / License / etc.]
• Value: [Total contract value]
• Term: [Initial term + renewal terms]
• Auto-renewal: [Yes/No — notice period]
• Termination: [Convenience right? Cure period?]

TOP RISKS (action required)
🔴 [Critical risk 1]
   What: [Description]
   Where: [Clause reference]
   Impact: [What could happen]
   Recommendation: [What to negotiate]

🟡 [Medium risk 1]
   What: [Description]
   Where: [Clause reference]
   Impact: [What could happen]
   Recommendation: [What to negotiate]

NOTABLE TERMS
• [Term that's unusual or important but not necessarily a risk]
• [Term that deviates from standard]

COMPARISON TO OUR STANDARD POSITION
| Topic | Our Standard | This Contract | Gap |
|-------|-------------|---------------|-----|
| Liability cap | 12 months fees | [X] | [Description] |
| Data protection | EU processing | [X] | [Description] |
| IP ownership | Customer owns | [X] | [Description] |

RECOMMENDATION
[Accept as-is / Accept with minor changes / Negotiate before signing / Do not sign]

NEGOTIATION PRIORITIES (if applicable)
1. [Highest priority change]
2. [Second priority]
3. [Third priority]
```

**Tone:** Clear, visual (use risk indicators), focused on decisions. Assume the reader needs to decide whether to proceed with the contract.

## Adapting to Document Type

| Document Type | Primary Template | Secondary Template |
|---|---|---|
| New regulation | Compliance Impact Brief | Legal Analysis |
| Court decision | Legal Analysis | Executive Summary |
| Vendor contract | Contract Risk Summary | Legal Analysis |
| Internal policy | Compliance Impact Brief | Executive Summary |
| Legislative proposal | Executive Summary | Legal Analysis |
| Regulatory guidance | Compliance Impact Brief | Legal Analysis |

**Key principle:** Always ask "Who is this summary for?" before choosing the format. The same document may need different summaries for different audiences.
