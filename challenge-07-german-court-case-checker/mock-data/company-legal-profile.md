## Key Legal Areas of Interest

The following legal topics are most relevant to TechForward's business and should be monitored for relevant court decisions:

### 1. IT & Software Law (IT-Recht)
- SaaS contract qualification (Mietrecht vs. Werkvertrag vs. Dienstvertrag)
- Software defect liability and warranty claims (Mängelhaftung)
- Service Level Agreements (SLA) and availability guarantees
- Open-source software licensing and compliance
- Liability for cloud service outages and data loss

### 2. Data Protection (Datenschutzrecht)
- GDPR enforcement decisions and fines in Germany
- Data processing agreements (Auftragsverarbeitung, Art. 28 DSGVO)
- International data transfers (Drittlandtransfer) post-Schrems II
- Employee data processing and monitoring (Beschäftigtendatenschutz)
- Data breach notification obligations

### 3. Employment Law (Arbeitsrecht)
- Remote work / home office regulations (Homeoffice / mobiles Arbeiten)
- Non-compete clauses (nachvertragliches Wettbewerbsverbot)
- Employee invention rights (Arbeitnehmererfindungsgesetz)
- Works council co-determination rights (Mitbestimmung) regarding AI and monitoring tools
- Temporary employment and contractor classification (Scheinselbständigkeit)

### 4. General Contract Law (Vertragsrecht / AGB-Recht)
- Validity of limitation of liability clauses in B2B contracts
- AGB-Kontrolle of standard contract terms (§§ 305–310 BGB)
- Force majeure and frustration of purpose (Störung der Geschäftsgrundlage, § 313 BGB)
- Penalties and liquidated damages clauses (Vertragsstrafe)

### 5. Intellectual Property (Gewerblicher Rechtsschutz)
- Software copyright and protection scope (Urheberrecht)
- AI-generated works and authorship
- Patent infringement in software and business methods
- Trade secret protection (Geschäftsgeheimnisgesetz)

### 6. Competition & Regulatory
- Digital Markets Act and platform regulation
- Antitrust implications of data sharing and API access
- Unfair competition (UWG) in digital marketing and SaaS sales
