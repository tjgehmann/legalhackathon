# TechForward Solutions GmbH — Standard Contract Positions

This document defines TechForward's standard contractual positions across key clause categories. These are the default terms in TechForward's template agreements. Any deviation from these positions is a "negotiation point."

---

## 1. Limitation of Liability

**Standard Position:**
- General liability cap: **12 months' fees** paid or payable under the agreement
- Statutory liability for intentional misconduct (Vorsatz) and gross negligence (grobe Fahrlässigkeit) remains unaffected
- Liability for injury to life, body, or health remains unlimited
- No liability for indirect, consequential, or incidental damages (including lost profits)

**Acceptable Range (without legal escalation):**
- Cap up to **18 months' fees**: Sales can approve
- Cap above 18 months' or separate carve-outs: Requires legal approval
- Uncapped liability or floor amounts: Requires VP Legal approval

---

## 2. Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

**Standard Position:**
- Standard DPA aligned with Art. 28 GDPR
- General authorization for sub-processors; current list published on TechForward's website
- TechForward provides 30 days' notice before adding new sub-processors
- Data processed in **Frankfurt (AWS)** and **Dublin (Azure)**
- Standard security measures documented in Annex II of DPA

**Acceptable Range (without legal escalation):**
- Specific authorization with reasonable objection period (30 days): Sales can approve
- Data residency restricted to EU: Sales can approve
- Customer-specific DPA language or sector-specific addenda: Requires legal review
- Restriction on specific sub-processors (e.g., US-based): Requires legal approval
- On-site audit rights: Requires legal approval

---

## 3. SLA / Uptime Guarantee

**Standard Position:**
- **99.5%** monthly uptime commitment (excluding scheduled maintenance)
- Service credits: **5% of monthly fee** for each 0.5% below SLA, capped at **10% of monthly fee**
- Scheduled maintenance windows: Saturdays 02:00–06:00 CET with 48-hour notice
- No termination right for SLA failures

**Acceptable Range (without legal escalation):**
- Up to **99.7%** uptime: Sales can approve
- Service credits up to **15%** of monthly fee: Sales can approve
- Above 99.7% or termination rights for SLA failures: Requires legal approval
- Custom RPO/RTO commitments: Requires technical and legal review

---

## 4. Auto-Renewal

**Standard Position:**
- Contracts auto-renew for successive **1-year terms**
- **90-day** written notice required to prevent renewal
- Price adjustments of up to **5%** permitted on renewal with 60-day notice

**Acceptable Range (without legal escalation):**
- Notice period reduced to **60 days**: Sales can approve
- Notice period reduced to **30 days**: Requires sales director approval
- No auto-renewal: Requires VP Sales approval (revenue retention concern)

---

## 5. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

**Standard Position:**
- **German law** applies, excluding UN CISG
- Exclusive jurisdiction: **Berlin courts**

**Acceptable Range (without legal escalation):**
- German law with different German court: Sales can approve
- German law with international arbitration (ICC, LCIA, Swiss Rules): Requires legal approval
- Non-German governing law: Requires VP Legal approval (rare — strong push to maintain German law)

---

## 6. Indemnification

**Standard Position:**
- **Mutual indemnification** for:
  - IP infringement claims (each party indemnifies for its own infringement)
  - Breach of confidentiality obligations
- TechForward's indemnification obligation capped at **12 months' fees**
- Customer must provide prompt notice, cooperation, and sole control of defense

**Acceptable Range (without legal escalation):**
- Uncapped IP indemnification: Requires legal approval
- Indemnification for regulatory fines or data breaches: Requires VP Legal approval
- One-sided indemnification (TechForward only): Requires VP Legal approval

---

## 7. Termination

**Standard Position:**
- **No termination for convenience** during initial contract term
- Termination for cause with **30-day cure period** for material breach
- Extraordinary termination for insolvency, force majeure lasting >90 days, or material breach

**Acceptable Range (without legal escalation):**
- Extended cure period (up to 60 days): Sales can approve
- Termination for convenience after initial term with 6+ months' notice: Requires legal approval
- Termination for convenience during initial term: Requires VP Legal and CFO approval
- Early termination fees: Minimum 30% of remaining contract value

---

## 8. IP Ownership

**Standard Position:**
- TechForward **retains all intellectual property** in the platform, including all improvements, updates, and derivative works
- Customer receives a **non-exclusive, non-transferable license** to use the platform during the subscription term
- Customer **retains ownership** of their data at all times

**Acceptable Range (without legal escalation):**
- Perpetual license for custom configurations: Sales can approve
- Customer ownership of custom integrations developed by customer: Requires legal review
- Joint IP ownership or customer IP ownership of custom development: Requires VP Legal approval
- ML model ownership or derived data rights: Requires VP Legal and CTO approval

---

## 9. Confidentiality

**Standard Position:**
- Mutual confidentiality obligations during term and **3 years** after termination
- Standard exceptions (public domain, independent development, legally required disclosure)
- TechForward may reference the customer as a client (name and logo) unless customer objects

**Acceptable Range (without legal escalation):**
- Extended confidentiality period (up to 5 years): Sales can approve
- No reference right: Sales can approve
- Perpetual confidentiality: Requires legal review
- Liquidated damages for confidentiality breach: Requires legal approval

---

## 10. Source Code Escrow

**Standard Position:**
- **No source code escrow** offered as standard
- Available as premium add-on (EUR 15,000/year)

**Acceptable Range (without legal escalation):**
- Escrow with release upon insolvency only: Requires legal approval
- Escrow with release upon material breach: Requires VP Legal approval
- Direct source code access: Not available
