## 1. Return & Refund Policy

### 1.1 Standard Return Window
- Customers may return products within **14 calendar days** of delivery for any reason (EU Distance Selling Directive / Fernabsatzgesetz).
- Products must be in original packaging and unused condition for a full refund.
- Defective products may be returned regardless of condition within the return window.
- Digital products (software licenses, downloads) are excluded from the right of withdrawal once download/activation has commenced, provided the customer was informed and gave explicit consent.

### 1.2 Refund Processing
- Refunds are processed to the original payment method within **5-7 business days** of receiving the returned item.
- Shipping costs for returns due to defects or fulfillment errors are covered by TechForward.
- Shipping costs for change-of-mind returns are borne by the customer.

### 1.3 Extended Return (Goodwill)
- For products up to 6 months old with confirmed defects, CC agents may offer a replacement or store credit as a goodwill gesture.
- Goodwill replacements for products valued up to **EUR 200** may be authorized by the CC agent directly.
- Products valued above EUR 200 require manager approval.
- All goodwill resolutions require the customer to accept a Micro-Waiver before processing.

## 2. Warranty Policy

### 2.1 Manufacturer Warranty
- TechForward provides a **12-month manufacturer warranty** on all hardware products.
- The warranty covers manufacturing defects only — not accidental damage, misuse, or normal wear and tear.
- Warranty claims must include proof of purchase and a description/photo of the defect.

### 2.2 EU Legal Guarantee
- Under EU law (Consumer Sales Directive), consumers have a **2-year legal guarantee** for goods that are not in conformity with the contract.
- During the **first 12 months**, any defect is presumed to have existed at the time of delivery (burden of proof on TechForward).
- After 12 months, the customer may need to demonstrate that the defect is a conformity issue (burden of proof varies by jurisdiction).
- The legal guarantee is separate from and in addition to the manufacturer warranty.

### 2.3 Warranty Exclusions
- Accidental damage (drops, spills, impact)
- Unauthorized modifications or repairs
- Use in commercial/professional settings for products marketed as consumer products
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect functionality
- Dead pixels: fewer than 5 dead pixels on displays are considered within manufacturing tolerances (ISO 9241-307 Class II)

### 2.4 Warranty Resolution Options
1. **Repair** — first option offered; free of charge during warranty period
2. **Replacement** — offered if repair is not feasible or would take more than 14 business days
3. **Refund** — offered only if neither repair nor replacement is possible; pro-rated based on product age

## 4. Data Protection & Privacy (GDPR)

### 4.1 Data Subject Rights
TechForward complies with GDPR and German BDSG. Customers have the right to:
- **Access** (Art. 15): Receive a copy of all personal data held, in machine-readable format, within 30 days.
- **Rectification** (Art. 16): Correct inaccurate personal data.
- **Erasure** (Art. 17): Request deletion of personal data ("right to be forgotten").
- **Restriction** (Art. 18): Restrict processing in certain circumstances.
- **Portability** (Art. 20): Receive data in a structured, commonly used format.
- **Object** (Art. 21): Object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing.

### 4.2 Right to Erasure — Handling Procedure
1. Verify the customer's identity (email confirmation + account security question).
2. Check for **blocking conditions**:
   - Open orders or returns in progress → deletion must wait
   - Unpaid invoices → deletion must wait
   - Active legal disputes → consult Legal before proceeding
3. If no blocking conditions: process deletion within 30 days.
4. **Mandatory data retention exceptions** (data NOT deleted):
   - Tax-relevant data: retained for **10 years** (AO § 147)
   - Contract data: retained for the **statutory limitation period** (3 years per BGB § 195)
   - Data relevant to ongoing legal proceedings: retained until proceedings conclude
5. Send confirmation to customer listing what was deleted and what was retained (with legal basis for each retained category).

### 4.3 Data Breach Response
- In the event of a data breach, affected customers must be notified **within 72 hours** if the breach poses a risk to their rights and freedoms (GDPR Art. 33/34).
- CC agents must NOT speculate about the scope or impact of a breach beyond what is stated in the official notification.
- All breach-related compensation requests must be escalated to Legal (GDPR Art. 82).
- CC agents may confirm factual information already disclosed in the official breach notification but should not provide additional details.

## 5. Service Level & Outage Policies

### 5.1 Consumer Plans
- Consumer plans do **not** include an SLA.
- TechForward aims for 99.5% uptime but makes no guarantees for consumer plans.
- Service credits for outages are offered as **goodwill gestures**, not contractual obligations.
- Standard goodwill credit: equivalent to 1 month of the customer's subscription (capped at EUR 50).

### 5.2 Business Plans
- Business plans include a **99.5% monthly uptime SLA**.
- Service credits for SLA breaches: 5% of monthly fee per hour of downtime, capped at 30% of monthly fee.
- Scheduled maintenance windows (communicated 48 hours in advance) are excluded from uptime calculations.

### 5.3 Enterprise Plans
- Enterprise SLAs are defined in individual contracts, typically guaranteeing **99.9% uptime**.
- Contractual penalties for SLA breaches vary by agreement.
- All enterprise SLA disputes must be escalated to Legal and the dedicated Account Manager.

### 5.4 Consequential Damages
- TechForward's liability for consequential, indirect, or special damages is **excluded** in the General Terms & Conditions for all plan types.
- This exclusion may not be enforceable against consumers under German law (BGB § 309 Nr. 7) for damages arising from injury to life, body, or health, or from grossly negligent or intentional breach of duty.
- Any claim involving consequential damages must be escalated to Legal.

## 7. Product Safety

### 7.1 Safety Reports
- Any report of a product causing injury or property damage must be escalated **immediately** to:
  1. CC Team Lead (within 1 hour)
  2. Product Safety Team (within 4 hours)
  3. Legal Department (within 4 hours)
- The customer should be asked to discontinue use of the product immediately.
- A prepaid return label must be issued for the product to be inspected.

### 7.2 Do's and Don'ts for CC Agents
- **DO**: Express concern, offer to arrange return, escalate immediately
- **DO**: Document the customer's description in detail, including photos if available
- **DO NOT**: Admit fault or liability on behalf of TechForward
- **DO NOT**: Speculate about the cause of the issue
- **DO NOT**: Promise specific outcomes (recall, compensation, etc.)

## 8. Escalation Guidelines

### 8.1 When to Escalate to Legal
Always escalate to Legal when:
- A customer mentions an attorney or threatens legal action
- Financial exposure exceeds EUR 1,000
- The issue involves potential product liability or safety
- A data breach or GDPR violation is alleged
- An enterprise contract dispute arises
- The CC agent is unsure whether the company's legal obligations apply

### 8.2 Escalation Package
When escalating, provide:
1. Customer details (name, ID, tier, account history)
2. Issue summary (what happened, what the customer wants)
3. Financial exposure (claimed amount + potential liability)
4. Relevant correspondence (chat logs, emails, photos)
5. Actions already taken by CC
6. Recommended resolution (CC agent's suggestion)
7. Urgency level and any deadlines (e.g., GDPR 30-day response requirement)

### 8.3 Response Time Targets
| Priority | Target Response | Examples |
|----------|----------------|----------|
| Critical | 4 hours | Product safety, active litigation, data breach |
| High | 1 business day | Enterprise disputes, GDPR requests, amounts > EUR 5,000 |
| Standard | 3 business days | Warranty disputes, subscription refunds, general legal questions |
| Low | 5 business days | Policy clarification, template updates |
