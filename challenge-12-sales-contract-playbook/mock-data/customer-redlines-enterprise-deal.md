# Customer Redlines — Enterprise Deal

**Deal:** GlobalManufacturing AG — Enterprise Software Services Agreement
**Deal Value:** EUR 480,000 / year (3-year contract, EUR 1.44M total)
**Customer Type:** Large enterprise, German manufacturing company, 12,000 employees
**Account Executive:** Jana Hoffmann
**Date of Redlines Received:** 2026-02-18
**Negotiation Status:** First round of customer redlines received

---

> The following redlines represent changes GlobalManufacturing AG's legal team has requested to TechForward's standard contract (v3.1). Each section below shows the **original TechForward language**, the **customer's proposed change**, and their **stated reason** (where provided).

---

## SECTION 3 — PAYMENT TERMS

### 3.3 Payment Due

**Original (TechForward):**
> Invoices are due and payable within **30 days** of the invoice date.

**Customer Redline:**
> Invoices are due and payable within **60 days** of the invoice date.

**Customer's Stated Reason:** "Our standard accounts payable process operates on a 60-day cycle. We cannot commit to 30-day payment for invoices of this size."

---

### 3.4 Late Payment

**Original (TechForward):**
> Amounts not paid by the due date will accrue interest at **1.5% per month**...

**Customer Redline:**
> Amounts not paid by the due date will accrue interest at **0.5% per month**...

**Customer's Stated Reason:** "1.5% per month is 18% per year — this is excessive and not market standard for enterprise B2B contracts."

---

### 3.5 Invoice Disputes

**Original (TechForward):**
> Customer may dispute an invoice in good faith within **21 days** of the invoice date...

**Customer Redline:**
> Customer may dispute an invoice in good faith within **45 days** of the invoice date...

**Customer's Stated Reason:** "Our internal invoice verification process takes up to 45 days given the number of approvers required for invoices of this size."

---

### 3.6 Price Adjustments

**Original (TechForward):**
> TechForward may adjust subscription fees once per year with **90 days' prior written notice**. Annual fee increases are capped at **8% per year**.

**Customer Redline:**
> TechForward may adjust subscription fees once per year with **180 days' prior written notice**. Annual fee increases are capped at **3% per year** or the HICP (Harmonised Index of Consumer Prices) for Germany, whichever is lower.

**Customer's Stated Reason:** "We are committing to a 3-year deal and need budget certainty. Unlimited 8% increases every year could cost us EUR 100K+ in year 3 above the agreed deal value."

---

## SECTION 4 — TERM AND TERMINATION

### 4.2 Auto-Renewal

**Original (TechForward):**
> ...the Agreement will automatically renew for successive **12-month periods**... non-renewal notice at least **60 days** before the end of the then-current Subscription Term.

**Customer Redline:**
> DELETE auto-renewal provision. Replace with: "This Agreement will not automatically renew. TechForward shall notify Customer **90 days** before the expiry of the Subscription Term, and the parties may negotiate renewal terms at that time."

**Customer's Stated Reason:** "We do not accept auto-renewal clauses in any vendor contracts. Our procurement policy requires explicit renewal approval."

---

### 4.4 Termination for Convenience

**Original (TechForward):**
> Customer may terminate this Agreement without cause upon **90 days' written notice**. In such case, all prepaid, unused fees for the remaining Subscription Term are **non-refundable**...

**Customer Redline:**
> Customer may terminate this Agreement without cause upon **30 days' written notice**. In such case, TechForward shall refund to Customer all prepaid, unused fees for the remaining Subscription Term within **30 days** of termination, on a pro-rata basis.

**Customer's Stated Reason:** "We are not prepared to be locked in with no exit. If the software doesn't meet our needs, we should be able to exit without forfeiting EUR 960K in prepaid fees."

---

### 4.5 Data Export

**Original (TechForward):**
> Customer may export Customer Data for **45 days** post-termination...

**Customer Redline:**
> Customer may export Customer Data for **180 days** post-termination...

**Customer's Stated Reason:** "45 days is not enough time for a company of our size to migrate data. We process over 2TB of operational data through this platform."

---

## SECTION 5 — WARRANTIES AND SERVICE LEVELS

### 5.1 Uptime SLA

**Original (TechForward):**
> ...the Software is available **99.5% of the time** per calendar month...

**Customer Redline:**
> ...the Software is available **99.9% of the time** per calendar month...

**Customer's Stated Reason:** "Our manufacturing operations rely on this software for real-time scheduling. 99.5% means up to 3.6 hours of downtime per month. We need 99.9% (max ~44 minutes/month)."

---

### 5.2 SLA Remedy

**Original (TechForward):**
> ...service credit equal to **5% of the monthly fee** for each 0.5% of downtime... up to a maximum of **25% of the monthly fee** for that month. Service credits are Customer's **sole remedy** for SLA failures.

**Customer Redline:**
> ...service credit equal to **10% of the monthly fee** for each 0.1% of downtime below 99.9%... up to a maximum of **50% of the monthly fee** for that month. If SLA performance falls below 99.0% in any rolling 3-month period, Customer may terminate this Agreement for cause without notice and receive a refund of all prepaid fees.

**Customer's Stated Reason:** "At EUR 40K/month, 25% credit = EUR 10K — less than our estimated operational losses for 3 hours of downtime. We need meaningful remedies."

---

## SECTION 6 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

### 6.3 Feedback

**Original (TechForward):**
> If Customer provides feedback or suggestions about the Software, TechForward may use such feedback **without restriction or obligation** to Customer.

**Customer Redline:**
> If Customer provides feedback or suggestions about the Software, TechForward may use such feedback solely for improving the Software under this Agreement. **TechForward shall not use Customer's feedback to develop competing products or offer improvements derived from Customer's feedback to Customer's competitors for a period of 3 years.**

**Customer's Stated Reason:** "We invest significant R&D in how we use this software. We don't want our operational innovations handed to our competitors."

---

### NEW CLAUSE — Source Code Escrow

**Customer Proposed Addition (new Section 6.5):**
> TechForward shall enter into a source code escrow agreement with an independent escrow agent within 60 days of the Effective Date. TechForward shall deposit the source code for the Software into escrow and update the deposit with each major release. The escrow shall be released to Customer in the event that TechForward: (a) ceases business operations; (b) enters insolvency proceedings; or (c) discontinues the Software with less than 12 months' notice.

**Customer's Stated Reason:** "For a mission-critical system, we need business continuity protection. If TechForward ceases operations, we cannot afford to lose access to the software."

---

## SECTION 7 — LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

### 7.2 Liability Cap

**Original (TechForward):**
> Each party's total aggregate liability... shall not exceed the total fees paid or payable by Customer in the **12 months** immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

**Customer Redline:**
> TechForward's total aggregate liability... shall not exceed the total fees paid or payable by Customer in the **36 months** immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim. Customer's total aggregate liability shall not exceed the total fees paid or payable in the **12 months** immediately preceding the event.

**Customer's Stated Reason:** "We are paying EUR 1.44M over 3 years. If TechForward causes a major failure in year 3, limiting liability to EUR 480K doesn't cover our potential losses."

---

### 7.3 Carve-Outs (Extension)

**Original (TechForward):**
> The exclusions and caps in Sections 7.1 and 7.2 do not apply to: (a) Customer's payment obligations; (b) either party's breach of confidentiality; (c) TechForward's IP infringement indemnification; (d) gross negligence or willful misconduct.

**Customer Redline:**
> ADD to carve-outs: **(e) TechForward's SLA failures where the Software is unavailable for more than 24 consecutive hours; (f) data breaches caused by TechForward's negligence or failure to maintain security standards.**

**Customer's Stated Reason:** "Extended outages and data breaches should not be capped — they can cause us regulatory fines, reputational damage, and operational losses far exceeding the contract value."

---

## SECTION 8 — DATA PROTECTION

### 8.3 Subprocessors

**Original (TechForward):**
> TechForward will provide **30 days' prior written notice** before adding new subprocessors. Customer may object within **15 days**.

**Customer Redline:**
> TechForward will provide **60 days' prior written notice** before adding new subprocessors. Customer may object within **30 days**. If Customer objects and TechForward cannot reasonably accommodate the objection, Customer may terminate the DPA and this Agreement without penalty.

**Customer's Stated Reason:** "We operate in a regulated environment. 15 days is not enough time for our IT security and legal team to assess a new subprocessor."

---

### 8.4 Data Breach Notification

**Original (TechForward):**
> TechForward will notify Customer of a confirmed personal data breach within **48 hours**...

**Customer Redline:**
> TechForward will notify Customer of a **suspected or confirmed** personal data breach within **24 hours** of becoming aware...

**Customer's Stated Reason:** "We need to report to our own regulators and boards within 72 hours. 48-hour notification from you leaves us only 24 hours to respond internally."

---

## SECTION 10 — GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

### 10.2 Dispute Resolution

**Original (TechForward):**
> Unresolved disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the **courts of Berlin, Germany**.

**Customer Redline:**
> Unresolved disputes shall be submitted to **ICC arbitration** with the seat of arbitration in **Frankfurt, Germany**, applying ICC Rules. The arbitral tribunal shall consist of one arbitrator agreed by the parties, or appointed by the ICC. The arbitration shall be conducted in **German**.

**Customer's Stated Reason:** "Litigation in Berlin courts is too slow and public. Our group policy requires arbitration for international contracts above EUR 500K."

---

## SECTION 9 — CONFIDENTIALITY

### 9.4 Publicity / Reference

**Original (TechForward):**
> Neither party may use the other party's name, logo, or trademarks in any marketing or publicity materials **without prior written consent**.

**Customer Redline:**
> TechForward may not use Customer's name, logo, or trademarks in any marketing, case studies, investor presentations, or publicity materials at any time, **for any reason**, without Customer's prior written consent in each instance.

**Customer's Stated Reason:** "We are a publicly listed company. Any mention of our name in vendor marketing could imply product endorsement and requires board-level approval. We have had issues with vendors citing us without permission."

---

## SECTION 11 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

### NEW CLAUSE — Most Favored Customer

**Customer Proposed Addition (new Section 11.7):**
> TechForward represents that the pricing and commercial terms offered to Customer under this Agreement are no less favorable than those offered to any other customer of comparable size and commitment. If TechForward subsequently offers more favorable terms to a comparable customer, TechForward shall promptly notify Customer and offer Customer the same or equivalent terms.

**Customer's Stated Reason:** "We are one of TechForward's largest customers. We want assurance we are not paying above-market rates or receiving less favorable terms than smaller customers."

---

*End of Customer Redlines — GlobalManufacturing AG — First Round*
