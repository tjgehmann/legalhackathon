# TechForward Solutions GmbH — Standard Software Services Agreement

**Version:** 3.1 (January 2026)
**Document type:** Outbound Sales Contract (Customer is the Buyer)

> This is TechForward's standard outbound contract used when selling its software platform and professional services to customers. When a customer proposes changes ("redlines"), this document is the baseline.

---

## SECTION 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 **"Agreement"** means this Software Services Agreement together with all Statements of Work, Order Forms, and incorporated schedules.

1.2 **"Software"** means TechForward's proprietary software platform, including updates and documentation, as described in the applicable Order Form.

1.3 **"Professional Services"** means implementation, configuration, training, and advisory services as described in a Statement of Work (SOW).

1.4 **"Customer Data"** means all data submitted by or on behalf of the Customer to the Software.

1.5 **"Confidential Information"** means any non-public information disclosed by either party that is designated as confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be confidential.

---

## SECTION 2 — LICENSE GRANT

2.1 **Software License.** TechForward grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable license to use the Software during the Subscription Term solely for Customer's internal business purposes.

2.2 **Restrictions.** Customer may not: (a) sublicense, resell, or distribute the Software; (b) reverse-engineer or decompile the Software; (c) use the Software to build a competing product; or (d) remove proprietary notices.

2.3 **Professional Services Work Product.** All deliverables produced under a Statement of Work that are created specifically for the Customer ("Custom Work Product") shall be owned by the Customer upon full payment of all applicable fees. TechForward retains ownership of all pre-existing IP, generic tools, methodologies, and know-how ("Background IP"). TechForward is granted a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use Customer's Custom Work Product solely for internal purposes.

---

## SECTION 3 — PAYMENT TERMS

3.1 **Fees.** Customer shall pay the fees set out in the applicable Order Form. All fees are in EUR and exclusive of VAT.

3.2 **Invoicing.** TechForward will invoice Customer as specified in the Order Form. For annual subscriptions, invoicing is annually in advance. For milestone-based professional services, invoicing is upon completion of each milestone.

3.3 **Payment Due.** Invoices are due and payable within **30 days** of the invoice date.

3.4 **Late Payment.** Amounts not paid by the due date will accrue interest at **1.5% per month** (or the maximum rate permitted by law, if lower) from the due date until paid. TechForward may suspend services if an invoice remains unpaid for 14 days after written notice.

3.5 **Disputes.** Customer may dispute an invoice in good faith within **21 days** of the invoice date by providing written notice specifying the disputed amounts. Disputed amounts will not accrue late interest pending resolution. Customer must pay undisputed amounts by the original due date.

3.6 **Price Adjustments.** TechForward may adjust subscription fees once per year with **90 days' prior written notice**. Annual fee increases are capped at **8% per year**.

---

## SECTION 4 — TERM AND TERMINATION

4.1 **Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for the initial Subscription Term specified in the Order Form (typically 12 or 24 months).

4.2 **Auto-Renewal.** Unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least **60 days** before the end of the then-current Subscription Term, the Agreement will automatically renew for successive **12-month periods**.

4.3 **Termination for Cause.** Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other party (a) materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within **30 days** of written notice specifying the breach, or (b) becomes insolvent or makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors.

4.4 **Termination for Convenience.** Customer may terminate this Agreement without cause upon **90 days' written notice**. In such case, all prepaid, unused fees for the remaining Subscription Term are **non-refundable**, except where TechForward has materially failed to deliver agreed services.

4.5 **Effect of Termination.** Upon termination: (a) all licenses cease; (b) Customer may export Customer Data for **45 days** post-termination using the standard export tool; (c) TechForward will delete Customer Data within 90 days.

---

## SECTION 5 — WARRANTIES AND SERVICE LEVELS

5.1 **Uptime SLA.** TechForward will use commercially reasonable efforts to ensure the Software is available **99.5% of the time** per calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance windows (communicated 48 hours in advance).

5.2 **SLA Remedy.** If TechForward fails to meet the uptime SLA, Customer is entitled to a **service credit** equal to 5% of the monthly fee for each 0.5% of downtime below the SLA target, up to a maximum of **25% of the monthly fee** for that month. Service credits are Customer's sole remedy for SLA failures.

5.3 **Warranty.** TechForward warrants that (a) the Software will perform materially in accordance with its documentation and (b) Professional Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner. TechForward will use commercially reasonable efforts to remedy any warranty breach notified in writing within 30 days of discovery.

5.4 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET OUT IN THIS SECTION 5, THE SOFTWARE AND SERVICES ARE PROVIDED "AS IS." TECHFORWARD DISCLAIMS ALL IMPLIED WARRANTIES, INCLUDING WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY AND FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

---

## SECTION 6 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

6.1 **TechForward IP.** TechForward retains all rights in the Software, its underlying technology, and all Background IP. Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of TechForward's IP to Customer.

6.2 **Customer Data.** Customer retains all rights in Customer Data. Customer grants TechForward a limited license to use Customer Data solely to provide the Software and Services.

6.3 **Feedback.** If Customer provides feedback or suggestions about the Software, TechForward may use such feedback without restriction or obligation to Customer. Customer retains no rights in any improvements made based on such feedback.

6.4 **IP Infringement Indemnification.** TechForward will defend and indemnify Customer against third-party claims that the Software (as delivered) infringes a third party's IP rights, provided Customer: (a) promptly notifies TechForward; (b) gives TechForward control of the defense; and (c) provides reasonable cooperation. This indemnification does not apply to claims arising from Customer modifications, third-party integrations, or use outside the scope of this Agreement.

---

## SECTION 7 — LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

7.1 **Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, EXEMPLARY, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES (INCLUDING LOST PROFITS, LOSS OF DATA, OR BUSINESS INTERRUPTION), EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.

7.2 **Liability Cap.** EACH PARTY'S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING UNDER OR IN CONNECTION WITH THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER IN THE **12 MONTHS** IMMEDIATELY PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.

7.3 **Carve-Outs.** The exclusions and caps in Sections 7.1 and 7.2 do not apply to: (a) Customer's payment obligations; (b) either party's breach of confidentiality obligations; (c) TechForward's IP infringement indemnification; (d) damages arising from gross negligence or willful misconduct.

---

## SECTION 8 — DATA PROTECTION

8.1 **Roles.** Customer is the data controller; TechForward is the data processor for Customer Data processed through the Software.

8.2 **DPA.** The parties will enter into a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) as a schedule to this Agreement, incorporating standard contractual clauses (SCCs) as required by applicable law.

8.3 **Subprocessors.** TechForward maintains a list of approved subprocessors. TechForward will provide **30 days' prior written notice** before adding new subprocessors. Customer may object to new subprocessors on data protection grounds within 15 days of notification.

8.4 **Data Breach Notification.** TechForward will notify Customer of a confirmed personal data breach within **48 hours** of becoming aware, with a preliminary report and a full report within 72 hours.

8.5 **International Transfers.** TechForward will only transfer Customer Data outside the EEA using lawful transfer mechanisms (SCCs, adequacy decisions, or BCRs).

---

## SECTION 9 — CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 **Obligations.** Each party agrees to keep the other party's Confidential Information confidential and not to disclose it to third parties without prior written consent. Each party may use the other's Confidential Information only as necessary to perform this Agreement.

9.2 **Duration.** Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this Agreement for **3 years**. Obligations relating to trade secrets survive indefinitely.

9.3 **Permitted Disclosures.** A party may disclose Confidential Information to its employees and professional advisors who need to know it, provided they are bound by equivalent obligations. Either party may disclose Confidential Information as required by law or court order, provided it gives the other party prompt notice and cooperates to seek protective measures.

9.4 **Publicity.** Neither party may use the other party's name, logo, or trademarks in any marketing or publicity materials without prior written consent.

---

## SECTION 10 — GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

10.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the **Federal Republic of Germany**, excluding conflict of law provisions.

10.2 **Dispute Resolution.** The parties agree to attempt to resolve disputes by senior management escalation within 30 days before initiating formal proceedings. Unresolved disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the **courts of Berlin, Germany**.

10.3 **Language.** This Agreement is in English. In the event of conflict between language versions, the English version prevails.

---

## SECTION 11 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

11.1 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with all incorporated schedules, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties on this subject matter.

11.2 **Amendments.** Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.

11.3 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other, except to an affiliate or a successor entity in a merger or acquisition. TechForward will provide Customer with 30 days' prior written notice of any assignment. Customer may terminate within 30 days of notice if the assignee is a direct competitor.

11.4 **Force Majeure.** Neither party is liable for delays or failures caused by events beyond its reasonable control (including natural disasters, pandemics, cyberattacks, government orders), provided the affected party gives prompt written notice. If a force majeure event continues for more than **90 days**, either party may terminate with 14 days' written notice.

11.5 **Severability.** If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force.

11.6 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by email (with read receipt) or certified mail to the addresses on the Order Form.
