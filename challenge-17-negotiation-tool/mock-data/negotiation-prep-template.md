## 3. Our BATNA

**If negotiations fail completely and we reach no agreement, what do we do?**
> [Describe your best outside option — another vendor, another buyer, self-build, status quo, etc.]

**How attractive is this alternative, honestly?**
> [What are the real costs, risks, and timeline of the alternative?]

**Could we strengthen our BATNA before or during negotiations?**
> [Steps we could take to improve our outside option]

**What is our reservation point — the minimum outcome we'd accept before walking away?**
> [Be specific per issue where possible]

## 6. Strategy & Sequencing

### Opening Move
> [What do we lead with? What do we anchor on?]

### Concession Strategy
> [What do we give first? What do we hold back? What signals strength vs. weakness?]

### Key Messages
> [What 2–3 things do we most need the counterparty to understand or believe?]

### Red Lines
> [What will we absolutely not agree to, regardless of what else is offered?]

### Walk-Away Plan
> [If negotiations reach our reservation point, how do we walk away cleanly while preserving the relationship?]

## AI Prompt to Generate Your Strategy Brief

Once you've filled in this template, paste it into your AI tool with the following instruction:

```
I've completed a negotiation preparation worksheet. Based on this information:

1. Assess the strength of our BATNA honestly — am I being realistic?
2. Identify where I may be wrong about the counterparty's real interests
3. Map the ZOPA for each issue — where does a deal become possible?
4. Suggest 3–5 value trades I haven't considered
5. Recommend an opening strategy, concession sequence, and walk-away approach
6. Flag any red flags or blind spots in my preparation

Apply the principles of principled negotiation (Fisher & Ury): separate people from problem, focus on interests not positions, invent options for mutual gain, use objective criteria.

Be direct. Challenge my assumptions. Tell me where I'm weak.
```
