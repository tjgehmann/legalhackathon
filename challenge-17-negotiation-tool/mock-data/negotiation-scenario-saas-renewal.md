# Negotiation Scenario: SaaS Platform Contract Renewal

## Context

**Our company:** Meridian AG — a mid-size German manufacturing company, ~1,200 employees  
**Counterparty:** Vanta Systems Inc. — a US-based SaaS vendor providing our ERP-integrated procurement platform  
**Deal type:** 3-year contract renewal  
**Current contract expires:** 90 days from now  
**Annual contract value (current):** €480,000/year

---

## The Issues on the Table

### Issue 1: Pricing

| | Our Position | Counterparty Position |
|---|---|---|
| **Opening ask** | 15% price reduction (€408,000/year) | 12% price increase (€537,600/year) |
| **Stated justification** | We have evaluated two alternatives; market is competitive | Platform usage has grown 40%; significant new features added |

**What we know:** Vanta recently lost two similar-sized European clients to a competitor. Their renewal rate across DACH customers is reportedly under pressure.

---

### Issue 2: Contract Term

| | Our Position | Counterparty Position |
|---|---|---|
| **Opening ask** | 1-year term (flexibility to switch if needed) | 3-year term (revenue predictability) |
| **Stated justification** | We want optionality while we evaluate the market | Longer term justifies investment in our integration |

---

### Issue 3: SLA and Uptime Guarantee

| | Our Position | Counterparty Position |
|---|---|---|
| **Opening ask** | 99.9% uptime SLA with financial penalties (1% credit per hour of downtime above threshold) | 99.5% uptime, no financial penalties — only service credits |
| **Stated justification** | Two outages last year cost us €40,000 in operational disruption | Financial penalties are not standard in our contracts |

---

### Issue 4: Data Portability & Exit Rights

| | Our Position | Counterparty Position |
|---|---|---|
| **Opening ask** | Full data export in machine-readable format within 30 days of termination; 6-month post-termination read-only access | 30-day export window; no post-termination access |
| **Stated justification** | Migration risk is high; we need continuity | Standard terms |

---

### Issue 5: Liability Cap

| | Our Position | Counterparty Position |
|---|---|---|
| **Opening ask** | Liability capped at 2x annual contract value | Liability capped at 1x annual contract value (current standard) |
| **Stated justification** | Potential data breach or outage impact exceeds 1x ACV | Risk management policy; cannot exceed 1x ACV |

---

## Our BATNA (as currently understood)

We have evaluated two alternatives:

1. **Procurix (German competitor):** Quoted €390,000/year; strong local data residency; however, migration would take 6–9 months and cost an estimated €120,000–€180,000 in implementation and downtime
2. **Build internal module:** IT estimates 18–24 months to build; €600,000+ investment; high execution risk

**BATNA strength assessment:** Moderate. Procurix is real but expensive when migration costs are factored in. The total cost of switching in Year 1 would be ~€510,000–€570,000, which is comparable to staying with Vanta at current or slightly increased pricing.

---

## Known Counterparty Constraints

- Vanta is preparing for a Series C funding round; ARR growth is a key metric for investors
- Their European sales team missed Q3 targets; losing Meridian AG would be significant for the team
- They have a new "Enterprise Success" program launching in Q1 that requires anchor clients for case studies
- Their engineering team is stretched; custom SLA commitments require VP-level approval

---

## Open Questions for Negotiation Prep

1. How strong is our BATNA really once migration costs are included? Should we strengthen it before the negotiation?
2. What does Vanta actually need most — is it the ACV, the term length, or a reference client relationship?
3. Where is the ZOPA on price? On term?
4. What creative trades could we offer that cost us little but are valuable to Vanta?
5. What's our walk-away point and are we prepared to actually walk?
