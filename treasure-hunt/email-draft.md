Dear participants,

We're looking forward to seeing you at the Legal Hackathon!

Before we dive into the challenges, we've prepared a short warm-up exercise to help you get comfortable with the AI tools you'll be using throughout the day: **an AI Treasure Hunt**.

**What is it?**

A 30-minute team exercise with 4 stations. Each station gives you a realistic legal task — reviewing a contract, querying a data set, structuring a memo, analyzing an email thread — and you'll use Gemini and Google Workspace to solve it. Complete all 4 stations, collect the code words, and unlock the link to the hackathon challenge repository.

**How does it work?**

1. Open the attached file **treasure-hunt.html** in your browser (just double-click it — no installation needed)
2. Follow the instructions for each station
3. Each station will point you to a shared Google Doc, Sheet, or Gemini itself
4. Solve the task, enter the code word on the page, and move to the next station
5. When all 4 are complete, the page reveals the link to the hackathon challenges

**What you'll need**

- A laptop with access to your Google Workspace account
- Gemini enabled in your Google Workspace (the sidebar in Docs, Sheets, etc.)
- Access to gemini.google.com
- Your team (2–4 people)

**What you'll learn**

Each station teaches a skill you'll use during the hackathon:

- **Station 1** — Using Gemini to find hidden risks in a long contract
- **Station 2** — Using Gemini to query a contract portfolio for urgent compliance issues
- **Station 3** — Using Gemini to turn chaotic input into a structured legal memo
- **Station 4** — Using Gemini to identify who made binding commitments in a negotiation thread

No prior AI experience is needed. The treasure hunt is designed to get you started — by the end, you'll be ready to tackle the real challenges.
